Who has access

Superadmins sign in with a name and a password and see every app. Users sign in with an access word of their own and see only the apps you say yes to for them.

People

To add a user, give them a name — so you know who is who — and an access word; the word is all they type to sign in, so no two users can share one. Show word tells you a user's word, to pass on; each look is written into the sign-ins below. Press Apps beside a user and choose, app by app: No, or Reader or Writer — which of the app's two accounts it opens as for them. An app with no sign-in of its own is just yes or no. An app someone may not see is not sent to their browser at all — not its name, not its address. Adding a name that already exists gives it a new word or password, and signs them out wherever they used the old one.

Sign-in keys

How an app signs a user straight in as its reader or writer account, with no email or password: the dashboard hands it a pass, signed with that app's own key. Show key, then put the key in the app's settings as UNISIS_SSO_KEY; once the app's sign-in page is live, add sso: '/sso' to it in apps.js. Until then the app opens as it always has. Each look is logged.
